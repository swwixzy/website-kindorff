About Kindorf

A structure built to move around ideas — not the other way around.

A person comes to us with an idea. We review the project, and if we take it on, KINDORF connects to its realization and provides the help it needs through our departments.

Our permanent departments — Management, Project Management, Research / Analytics, SMM / Media, Partnerships, IT / Technical, Design, Finance, and Legal — don't become a dedicated team for every single project. Instead, they connect to projects as needed: a study that needs to be published reaches out to Research / Analytics; a project that needs an international partner reaches Partnerships.

  • Purpose

    Help people realize their own projects, rather than running projects for them.

  • Principle

    Departments are shared infrastructure. They plug into a project only when it needs them.

  • Model

    Idea → review → if accepted, a Project Manager and the right departments connect around it.
